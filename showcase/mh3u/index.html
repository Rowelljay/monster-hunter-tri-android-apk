Quick setup

  1. Install the APK. If your own decrypted USA 3DS game comes in a ZIP or 7Z archive, extract it first.
  2. Open the app and allow file access. Tap Choose folder, open the folder containing your extracted .3ds or .cci file, then tap Use this folder.
  3. Tap Build on this phone → Build. Keep your internet connection on for the first tool download. Tap Play when the build finishes.
