First time? Here’s the setup.
  1. Install or extract your app.Android: choose the APK matching your device’s supported architecture, then follow the installation prompt. Windows: extract the ZIP into its own folder and open the app.
  2. Choose your game file.Select your European/PAL RVZ or ISO. Extract ZIP/RAR archives first.
  3. Let the first import finish.Keep enough storage for the game and extracted data. Low Spec uses its own MH3_Low_Spec folder; existing saves stay with the original app. Use save export/import to transfer progress.

Android requires OpenGL ES 3.0. A 64-bit phone can run Low Spec only if its Android system supports 32-bit apps. Low Spec is experimental and does not guarantee compatibility or higher FPS. Standard-build solo speed is experimental; online play stays at normal speed.
